Apprentissage PAC-Bayes

This part of the course presents PAC-Bayesian theory, a theoretical framework which is particularly well suited to ensemble methods: a weighted majority vote (bagging, random forests, boosting...) is nothing but a distribution over voters. We will see how to derive tight generalization bounds (risk certificates) for such votes, how the diversity of the voters appears in these bounds (tandem bound, C-bound), and how to learn the weights of a vote by minimizing a bound. Kernel methods and linear classifiers are also revisited from this point of view.
